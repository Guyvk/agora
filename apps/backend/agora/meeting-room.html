This meeting writes its own minutes

While you are unmuted, Agora turns what you say into text. The notes are not shown during the meeting. When the meeting ends, the assistant writes the minutes and posts them in the circle.

  • Only your own microphone is used, and only while you are unmuted.
  • The sound is deleted as soon as it has become text. Only the text is kept.
  • You can pause your notes at any moment, for something off the record.
  • Next to the call there is a chat for everyone in the meeting. It is saved and goes into the minutes.

On a phone or a narrow screen the chat is not shown for now. To read or write in the chat, use a computer.

About the notes.

If you join without the notes, Agora never records your microphone: what you say only goes to the call.
