This meeting writes its own minutes

While you are unmuted, Agora turns what you say into text. When the meeting ends, the assistant writes the minutes and posts them in the group.

  • Only your own microphone is used, and only while you are unmuted.
  • The sound is deleted as soon as it has become text. Only the text is kept.
  • You can pause your notes at any moment, for something off the record.

About the notes.

If you join without the notes, Agora never records your microphone: what you say only goes to the call.
