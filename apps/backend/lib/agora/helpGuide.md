# More notes for Help (things the guide above does not say)

Claude in Help answers from the guide above, which is the same guide members read. These notes add what the Help desk often gets asked and the guide leaves out. When the guide and these notes differ, the guide is right. Do not repeat the guide here: change the guide instead (apps/web/src/agora/guide/guideParts.json and the language files).

## Getting around

- Agora is a website; there is no phone app and there are no push notifications.
- Hylo, the platform under Agora, calls circles "groups". A circle can have smaller spaces inside it.
- The bar on the left also has My Home (everything from your circles), Messages and a Create button.
- Your picture in the left bar opens a menu with your profile, your settings (name, picture, email, password, notifications, language) and Logout.
- Each circle has a stream of posts, chat rooms, members, events and a menu with its pages.

## Circles and stewards

- Stewards are the people who run a circle (in Hylo they have the Administration responsibility). They change its settings, invite people and approve requests to join.
- To join a circle, ask one of its stewards for an invitation or the join link.
- The 7BP Home is the main circle. Every circle sits inside it and every member of any circle is a member of it. It opens on a welcome page, with the announcements, the meetings and events, the members and a menu entry for each circle. Only its members can see it.

## Events and meetings

- To make an event, use the plus button or Create in a circle and choose Event. Invite people from the event.
- The notes after a meeting need speech to text to be set up on the server. When it is not, the meeting room says so and records nothing. A fault with the notes is worth passing on to the team.

## Contacts

- Every member can also add and delete contacts. "My follow-ups" lists the follow-ups that are yours.

## Account

- Forgot your password: on the login page, click "Forgot password?" and follow the email.
- Change your email, password, notifications or language: your picture in the left bar, then the settings.
- If someone is not sure where something is, they should say so: the team would rather hear about it than have them guess.

## Things Agora does not have (yet)

- No public area: everything is for members.
- No tracking or advertising.
