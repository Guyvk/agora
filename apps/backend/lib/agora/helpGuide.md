# How Agora works (the guide Claude answers from in the Help window)

Agora is the online home of 7 Billion Presidents (7BP), a movement of volunteers organised in circles. It is built on Hylo, an open source community platform. Agora is a website; there is no phone app.

## Getting around

- The bar on the left lists your circles (Hylo calls them groups). Click one to open it. A circle can have smaller spaces inside it.
- The left bar also has My Home (everything from your circles), Messages (private chats with one or more people), Contacts, a Create button, and notifications.
- Your picture in the left bar opens a menu with your profile, your settings (name, picture, email, password, notifications, language) and Logout.
- The Help button at the bottom right of every page opens Help: ask a question, report a fault or share a wish. "My requests" shows what you sent and the answers from the team.

## Circles

- Each circle has a stream of posts, chat rooms, members, events, and a menu with its pages.
- To join a circle you need an invitation or the circle's join link. Ask a steward of the circle.
- Stewards are the people who run a circle (they have the Administration responsibility). They change its settings, invite people and approve join requests.
- Posts can be discussions, requests, offers, resources, projects, proposals and events. Use the + button or "Create" in a circle to make one.
- Proposals let a circle decide: members vote, and the result shows on the post.

## Events and meetings

- Make an event in a circle with the + button and choose Event. Invite people from the event.
- Every event has a meeting room: open the event and choose "Meet in Agora, with minutes". The room asks whether your voice may be part of the notes. Use headphones.
- When the meeting ends, the assistant posts minutes in the circle: a summary, decisions, actions and open questions, with the transcript. This only works when speech to text is set up; otherwise the room says so.

## Files

- Each circle can have a Files page that shows its Google Drive folder. Files open in Google Drive. Members of the circle can add files and folders when that is switched on.

## Contacts

- The Contacts button in the left bar opens one shared list of the people and organisations 7BP works with outside Agora: partners, press, funders and interested people.
- Every Agora member can read, add, change and delete contacts. Notes keep what was said. A follow-up is a next step with a date and a person; "My follow-ups" lists yours, and the number on the button is how many are due.
- These are real people's details. Only add someone who agreed to be contacted, and write where the details came from.

## Playbook

- Each circle can have a Playbook page (in the circle's menu): every chapter of its playbook with an owner, a status (not started, draft, in review, agreed), a short text and a link. Stewards change it; members read it.

## Account

- Forgot your password: on the login page, click "Forgot password?" and follow the email.
- Change your email, password, notifications or language: your picture in the left bar, then the settings.
- If you are not sure where something is, say so: the team would rather hear about it than have you guess.
- Emails: Agora sends emails for invitations, mentions, new posts in circles you follow, and digests, depending on your notification settings.

## Things Agora does not have (yet)

- No phone app and no push notifications.
- No public area: everything is for members.
- No tracking or advertising.
